This answers your preview comment: *"finish the dusty wiring… come up from the
menu at the bottom on mobile and come in from the side in desktop… the context
of whatever I had open."* It follows your three choices: Claude, read and
answer only, and nothing saved.

## What it does

- **Opening it**
  - On a phone, tap the emblem in the middle of the footer and Dusty rises
    from the bottom.
  - On desktop, tap the emblem at the bottom of the left rail and Dusty slides
    in from the right. The page stays usable beside it.
- **It knows what you have open.** The header says which:
  - "On Sample Customer's thread": the contact and whether their name is
    confirmed, the last 80 messages, call summaries and next steps, the full
    transcripts of the three most recent calls, and their jobs.
  - "On scope: …": the sections and the calculation engine's own numbers. If
    the price is blocked, Dusty names what's blocking it. It never guesses a
    price.
  - "On … (customer page)": the person and their recent messages.
  - "On Cash and cost settings": each rate, recorded or blank, and your
    overhead items.
  - Anywhere else: "Cold — nothing open".
- **Changing screen with Dusty open** adds a line in the chat saying it's now
  looking at the new screen.
- **Starter questions** for each kind of screen, and **Copy** on every reply,
  so a draft can go straight into Quo.
- **It changes nothing and sends nothing**, and the conversation is gone when
  you reload the page.

## How it's put together

- **The browser only says which screen is open.** The server looks everything
  up itself under your login, with the database's own access rules applied.
  Nothing the browser sends is treated as fact.
- **The fixed instructions sit first and are cached**, so repeat questions
  cost less. The screen's data comes after them.
- **Dusty's standing rules:**
  - never invent a number, name or dimension; "not recorded" is an answer
  - a name the system guessed is a question for you, never a fact
  - customer vs sub is decided by who is asking whom for money or work
  - drafts are for you to send
- **Model:** `claude-opus-5`, set to medium effort to keep replies quick. If
  it declines a question, the request is retried on a fallback model
  automatically.
- **Limits:** you must be signed in; conversations are capped at 30 turns and
  a message at 4,000 characters.
- **Removed:** the old `/api/chat/dusty` route. Nothing called it. Anyone could
  reach it without logging in, and it pointed at a small model on a private
  home-network address that Vercel can't reach.

## What you do

1. **Add the key.** Create an API key at console.anthropic.com. In Vercel, go
   to scopecrete → Settings → Environment Variables and add it as
   **`ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`**:
   - mark it **Sensitive**
   - tick Preview and Production
   - don't give it a `NEXT_PUBLIC_` prefix
2. **Redeploy the preview.** Until then, Dusty says it isn't connected rather
   than pretending.
3. **Try it on a thread.** Ask "What does this person want?"
   - **Pass:** the answer quotes that thread.
4. **Try it on a scope.** Ask "What's the price?"
   - **Pass:** the answer matches the builder's takeoff panel, or names the
     blocked settings.

## Hermes (the model on the VPS)

- `/api/dusty` sends its requests to
  `${HERMES_URL}/v1/chat/completions` with `Authorization: Bearer ${HERMES_API_KEY}`.
  - Dusty's instructions and the screen's data go in the system message.
  - The reply streams back to the panel.
  - A plain JSON reply also works, and a timeout or error is shown as an error.
- **Vercel variables**, all marked Sensitive, for Preview and Production:
  - `HERMES_URL` = `https://srv1594563.tail0f2914.ts.net:8443` (Hermes alone on its own Funnel port; 443 is tailnet-only)
  - `HERMES_API_KEY` = Hermes's own access key (`X_HERMES_KEY`), not an
    Anthropic key
  - `HERMES_MODEL` is optional; the default is `anthropic/claude-3.5-haiku`
- **Checked locally** against a fake server that answers the same way Hermes
  does:
  - streamed text arrives
  - Dusty's instructions reach it
  - a plain JSON reply works
  - a 500 shows as "Hermes returned 500"
- **Not checked live.** The sandbox can't reach `*.ts.net`.
- **The Hermes key was pasted into chat, so rotate it.** Put a new
  `X_HERMES_KEY` in the container, and put the new value in Vercel yourself.
- **Hermes should answer Dusty with its tools off.** The context includes
  customer texts, and a text can carry instructions.
